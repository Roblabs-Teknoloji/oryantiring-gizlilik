Privacy Policy

Orienteering: Route Hunter (com.roblabs.oryantiring) · RobLabs · Last updated: November 2, 2026

Data we collect

This app does not collect, store or share any personal data with anyone.

Stored on your device

Your player name, game progress (stars, best times, experience points), language and graphics settings and the course leaderboards are stored only on your device. They are never sent to any server and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

Internet, ads and analytics

The app does not use the internet (it does not request the internet permission). It contains no ads, analytics, location tracking or in-app purchases.

Permissions

The app only uses the vibrate permission for a short vibration when a control is punched.

Children

Since the app collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page and the date above will be updated.
